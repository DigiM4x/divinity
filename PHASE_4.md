# Phase 4 — Miracles and Alignment

Same stack and rules. Sim on the fixed 20 Hz clock, nothing loaded from disk.

---

## What was built

**An event bus, first.** `state.events` is a ~20-line emit/subscribe map. This
closes the architectural crack flagged at the end of Phase 3: `props.js` and
`hand.js` were reaching directly into `creature.witness()`, which meant physics
knew the creature existed. They now emit facts — `villagers-killed`,
`building-destroyed`, `villagers-fed`, `prop-thrown`, `miracle-cast`,
`villager-born` — and the creature and the alignment system each subscribe. The
indirection immediately paid for itself twice: alignment needed exactly the same
feed, and Phase 5's rival towns will be a third consumer.

**Belief** is generated by villagers, scaled by town happiness. Happiness is a
readable blend of food per head, spare beds, and how many villagers are
currently hungry, eased so one bad tick can't crash production. Miracles cost
belief and nothing else, which gives the whole system its spine: **a cruel god
who starves his people loses the power to be cruel.**

**Gesture casting.** Hold right-mouse and draw. Recognition is the simple half
of the $1 recogniser: resample the path to 32 points, centre it on its
centroid, scale the longer axis to 1, and compare point-for-point against each
template. No rotation invariance — a bolt drawn upside down should not be a
bolt. Two guards matter as much as the matcher: a shape worse than
`MATCH_THRESHOLD` is **rejected outright** rather than firing the nearest wrong
miracle, and a best match that doesn't beat the runner-up by `MATCH_MARGIN` is
reported ambiguous. Doing nothing is much better than casting a fireball on your
own town because your circle was scruffy.

| Gesture | Miracle | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| ○ ring | Food | 30 | +45 food, feeds villagers in range |
| ∿ wave | Water | 20 | Boosts farm crops, washes the creature |
| ∧ caret | Fireball | 55 | Craters ground, razes buildings, kills, blasts props outward |
| Z bolt | Lightning | 45 | Tight strike: kills and flattens in a small radius |

The trail is drawn as an SVG overlay while you draw, and a grimoire panel in the
corner shows each glyph, its cost, and greys out what you can't afford — the
glyphs are rendered from the same template data the matcher uses, so the
reference can never drift out of sync with what's actually recognised.

**Alignment** is one float in −1..+1, moved by deeds on the event bus and by
miracles cast, eased so the world doesn't flicker. It drives no mechanics at
all; it only changes how things look:

- **Terrain** saturation shifts in the fragment shader — cruel land goes grey
  and cold, merciful land goes lush. Done in the shader patch that already
  existed for the influence ring, so it costs nothing extra.
- **Buildings** swap between two geometry variants past `GEOMETRY_SWAP_AT`.
  Houses go from squat red pyramid roofs to tall dark spiked ones; workshops
  grow a taller chimney. Crucially this is *one* builder function with an
  `evil` flag — the variants share walls, doors and proportions and differ only
  in a cone's height, a palette, and four corner spikes. Farms and storage pits
  keep one shape and change by tint alone. Swapping is a single geometry pointer
  per type; instance matrices are untouched.
- **The creature** darkens and reddens, with a faint glow in its eyes.
- **The hand** goes from warm flesh to ashen and red-lit.

**Opinion generalisation**, also promised at the end of Phase 3. A `KINSHIP`
table groups object types into families (stone, plant, person, structure) and
40% of every lesson leaks to relatives. Measured: one slap for eating a rock
gives `rock −0.80`, `boulder −0.32`, and leaves `tree` at `0.00`.

**Right-drag had to be given up** to gestures, so orbiting moved to middle-drag,
Alt+left-drag, and Q/E.

## Tunable constants

All in `src/state.js`.

| Constant | Value | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| `MIRACLE.BELIEF_PER_VILLAGER` | 0.055/s | Belief income at full happiness. The economy's master dial. |
| `TOWN.MIN_HAPPINESS` | 0.15 | Floor, so a wretched town still trickles belief. |
| `MIRACLE.MATCH_THRESHOLD` | 0.45 | Raise to accept sloppier drawing, lower to demand precision. |
| `MIRACLE.MATCH_MARGIN` | 0.02 | How far the best match must beat the runner-up before it fires. |
| `MIRACLE.RESAMPLE` | 32 | Points per normalised path. |
| `MIRACLE.MIN_PATH_LENGTH` | 90 px | Shorter strokes are ignored as stray clicks. |
| `MIRACLE.MIN_POINT_SPACING` | 6 px | Jitter rejection; duplicated points wreck resampling. |
| `MIRACLES.*.cost` | 20–55 | Per-miracle belief price. |
| `MIRACLES.*.align` | −0.06..+0.05 | What casting it does to your soul. |
| `MIRACLE.*_RADIUS` | 9–22 | Effect radius per miracle. |
| `ALIGNMENT.CRUSH_VILLAGER` | −0.07 | The heaviest single deed. |
| `ALIGNMENT.SMOOTH` | 0.7/s | How fast the world follows a change of heart. |
| `ALIGNMENT.GEOMETRY_SWAP_AT` | 0.3 | Where silhouettes flip. Kept off zero so it can't flicker. |
| `KINSHIP_LEAK` | 0.4 | Fraction of a lesson that reaches relatives. |
| `CAMERA.KEY_ROTATE_SPEED` | 1.5 rad/s | Q/E orbit rate. |

## Bugs found and fixed during the build

1. **Alt+left-drag silently failed to orbit.** The hand grabbed whatever was
   under the cursor and captured the button before the camera ever saw the
   drag — a direct consequence of the forgiving pick added last round, which
   made "there is something under the cursor" almost always true. Alt now
   suppresses grabbing as well as panning. Caught only because the camera
   rebinding was tested rather than assumed.
2. **The hand blew out to near-white when cruel.** Adding emissive on top of a
   darkened red albedo clips under ACES, producing a pale blob — the exact
   opposite of the intended ashen look. The glow now *decreases* toward evil.

## What I'd do differently

- **Alignment is cosmetic only.** The spec asked for that, and it's honest, but
  it leaves the moral axis inert: nothing mechanically rewards either pole. The
  obvious hook is belief itself — mercy should generate more of it, cruelty
  should generate less but let you take it by force. That tension is what makes
  the choice a choice rather than a skin.
- **Only two building types have an evil variant.** Houses and workshops swap
  silhouettes; farms and storage pits only recolour. That was deliberate scope
  control and it reads fine at gameplay zoom, but the town centre — the single
  biggest object on screen — doesn't change at all, which is the most
  conspicuous omission.
- **Gesture matching has no rotation invariance and no scale floor per shape.**
  Drawing a tiny bolt in the corner works exactly as well as a large one, which
  is convenient but means a careless flick can cast lightning. A minimum
  bounding-box size per miracle, scaled by its cost, would make the destructive
  ones require a deliberate, large gesture.
- **Miracles ignore rival towns entirely** because there aren't any yet. Every
  radius check walks `state.town.buildings` and `state.villagers.list` directly.
  Phase 5 will need those loops to iterate over *all* towns, which is the point
  at which villagers and buildings should stop being one flat global list.
- **The event bus has no payload schema.** It's a Map of string → callbacks, and
  a typo in an event name fails silently. At six event types that's fine; at
  twenty it won't be, and a frozen `EVENTS` constant object of names would cost
  nothing.
